Retail Sales & P&L Analytics Tool

A messy manual sales ledger, rebuilt into a self-updating financial tracker

A small retail shop was recording daily sales by hand with no automation. Two real data-quality problems were hiding in it: a "returns" column silently mixing refunds with unrelated store-credit redemptions, and holiday entries typed as text that broke every formula downstream. Rebuilt the whole thing into a 12-month tracker where every total is a live formula, added a forecasting layer that labels each month "Actual" or "Projected" on its own, and built a dashboard to make the difference visible at a glance.

what was found

  • A mixed-sign "returns" column was quietly combining two different real events into one number — split into two proper columns so nothing is miscounted.
  • Text entries like "Holiday" typed into number fields were silently breaking every formula in that row — replaced with a dedicated notes field.
  • Future months previously showed as blank zero — replaced with a trend-based estimate that automatically switches to "Actual" once real data is entered.
